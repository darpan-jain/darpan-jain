### Building scalable ML systems that turn research into reliable product experiences.
---

I'm a Lead Machine Learning Engineer at [Crunchyroll (Sony Pictures Entertainment)](https://darpanjain.com/#work-section), where I build recommendation and personalization systems—and a centralized AI platform—for more than 15 million anime fans. My work spans architectures for training and serving language and multimodal models across production use cases.

Previously, I was an Applied Researcher in Natural Language Processing at [USC Information Sciences Institute](https://www.isi.edu), where I worked on conversational AI for moderating harmful online discourse. That work led to a [NAACL 2024 paper on language-model moderators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.10781).
